Common Questions

Charleston Pressure Washing FAQ

How much does it cost to pressure wash a house in Charleston, SC?

Most Charleston homeowners pay $300–$700 for a full-house exterior soft wash, $150–$400 for driveway cleaning, and $400–$800 for roof soft washing. Final pricing depends on home size, height, surface type, and how much algae or organic growth is present. Broad Street Pressure Wash provides a free quote after a quick walk-around.

Is power washing the same as pressure washing?

In Charleston, power washing and pressure washing are used interchangeably for exterior cleaning of driveways, concrete, siding, and other hard surfaces. The technical difference is that power washing uses heated water; both refer to high-pressure exterior cleaning. Soft washing is a separate low-pressure method used on roofs and delicate siding.

What's the best month to pressure wash a house in Charleston?

For most Charleston homes, March through November is ideal because temperatures stay above freezing and humidity-driven algae growth is most visible. Spring after pollen season and fall before winter are the two best windows for full exterior cleaning.

Soft wash vs pressure wash — which does my Charleston home need?

Hard surfaces — concrete driveways, brick patios, sidewalks, gutter faces — handle traditional high-pressure washing well. Delicate surfaces — asphalt shingle roofs, vinyl siding, painted trim, stucco, and soffits — need low-pressure soft washing with a cleaning solution to lift algae and mildew without surface damage. Broad Street Pressure Wash uses both methods depending on the surface.
