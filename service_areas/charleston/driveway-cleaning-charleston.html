Know the Signs

Signs Your Driveway Is Due for Cleaning

  • Dark, blotchy concrete or black lines along control joints
  • Slick green algae in the shaded spots where the driveway never fully dries
  • Orange rust stains from irrigation water or fertilizer
  • Tire marks and oil shadows where cars park every day
  • Red clay and mud splashed along the edges after heavy rain

Lowcountry Conditions

Why Charleston Concrete Turns Dark

Concrete is porous. In a humid coastal climate it soaks up moisture, and mildew and algae grow in the pores, giving driveways that gray-black, uneven look. Shade from oak trees, sprinklers that hit the same spot every morning and pine straw left on the edges speed it up.

Many Lowcountry homes have exposed-aggregate driveways, brick ribbon borders or paver aprons. Each one needs a different approach. We use a rotary surface cleaner for an even finish without zebra stripes, adjust the pressure for aggregate and pavers, and rinse the debris off lawns and beds when we finish.

FAQ

Driveway Cleaning Charleston FAQ

These are common local questions people ask before booking driveway cleaning in Charleston, SC.

What does driveway cleaning include?

A standard driveway cleaning covers the full concrete driveway surface, the driveway apron (the section where the driveway meets the street), and the front entry walkway when requested. We use a flat-deck rotary surface cleaner that pulls grime evenly across the slab, then pressure-rinse the seams, edges, and any areas the surface cleaner can't reach. The result is a uniform finish — no zebra-striping, no missed strips along the joints, no etched surface from over-pressure.

What surfaces can you clean — concrete, aggregate, or brick driveways?

All three. We clean standard broom-finished concrete with a rotary surface cleaner at moderate PSI; exposed aggregate at a slightly lower pressure to avoid loosening the decorative stones; and brick or paver driveways with a soft-wash pre-treatment plus a controlled surface-cleaner pass. The method is matched to the material so the finish stays even and the driveway brightens without surface damage.

How often should a driveway be cleaned in Charleston, SC?

Most Charleston driveways need cleaning once a year — typically in spring after pollen season — to keep up with the algae, mildew, and surface darkening that builds in the Lowcountry's humid climate. Shaded driveways under oak canopy, driveways with frequent vehicle traffic, and rental properties between tenants often need cleaning every 6–9 months. Driveways in newer subdivisions with full sun exposure can sometimes go 18–24 months between washes.

How much does driveway cleaning cost in Charleston?

Most Charleston driveway cleaning jobs run $150–$400. Pricing depends on driveway size (most residential driveways are 400–800 square feet), surface type (concrete is fastest; aggregate and brick take longer), how much staining is present, and whether front walkways or aprons are included. Broad Street Pressure Wash provides a free walk-around quote so the price is locked in before any work starts.

Can you remove oil stains or rust marks?

Most surface staining lifts during a standard cleaning. Old, deep oil saturation and rust marks often lighten significantly but may not disappear completely — concrete is porous and absorbs oil over time. We'll set realistic expectations during the walk-around so you know what to expect before booking. Heavy oil stains sometimes need a separate degreasing pre-treatment, which we'll quote if needed.
