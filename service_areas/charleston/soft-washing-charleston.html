Lowcountry Conditions

Why Lowcountry Siding Gets Dirty So Fast

Charleston averages high humidity for most of the year, and mildew and algae grow wherever siding stays damp. Walls that face north or sit under live oaks and Spanish moss rarely dry out fully, so they go green first. Each spring a wave of pine and oak pollen coats every horizontal surface, then sticks to the algae already there.

Near the harbor, the marsh and the barrier islands, salt carried on the breeze leaves a dull film on siding, railings and glass. On historic homes on the peninsula, with painted wood clapboard, old brick and stucco, high pressure can lift paint, etch soft mortar and push water behind the boards. That is why we wash these surfaces at low pressure and let a cleaning solution do the work.

FAQ

Soft Washing Charleston FAQ

These are common questions homeowners ask before booking soft washing in Charleston, SC.

What is soft washing?

Soft washing is a low-pressure exterior cleaning method used on more delicate surfaces such as siding, trim, soffits, stucco, and other home exterior materials.

What surfaces can be soft washed in Charleston?

Soft washing is commonly used for siding, trim, soffits, painted exterior surfaces, stucco, fencing, and other home exterior areas that need a gentler cleaning approach.

Why do Charleston homes need soft washing?

Charleston homes deal with humidity, algae, mildew, pollen, and dirt buildup that collect quickly on exterior surfaces. Soft washing helps clean those surfaces while being appropriate for more delicate materials.

Soft washing vs pressure washing — what's the difference?

Pressure washing uses high water pressure to clean hard surfaces like concrete driveways, brick patios, and gutter faces. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution that lifts algae and mildew at the root — it's the right method for siding, trim, soffits, stucco, and asphalt shingle roofs that would be damaged by high pressure. We use each method on the surfaces it's right for.

How often should a Charleston home be soft washed?

In the Lowcountry climate, most Charleston homes benefit from a soft wash once a year because humidity, pollen, and shade-driven algae make exterior buildup visible within 12 months. Homes under heavy oak canopies or in shaded lots may need it more often; homes in sunnier, more exposed locations can sometimes go 18 months between washes.

How long does a soft wash last?

A professional soft wash typically holds for 12–18 months on Charleston-area homes before algae and mildew become visible again. Pairing the soft wash with gutter cleaning and roof cleaning extends how clean the home reads from the curb between full annual washes.
