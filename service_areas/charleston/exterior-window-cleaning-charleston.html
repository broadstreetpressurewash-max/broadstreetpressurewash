Outside Window Washing

Professional Exterior Window Washing for Charleston Homes

Outside windows in Charleston deal with constant exposure to pollen, salt air off the harbor, summer humidity, sprinkler overspray, and rain spotting. Over time, that buildup leaves exterior glass looking hazy, streaked, and dull — and from the street, dirty windows can drag down even a freshly painted home.

Professional exterior window cleaning restores a clearer, brighter finish to one of the most visible features on the home. We work all elevations, including upper-floor windows that aren't safely reachable on a stepladder, using a water-fed pole and purified water for a streak-free finish without ladders against fragile siding or trim.

Whether you're prepping for listing photos, pollen season is over, or you just want the house to read brighter from the curb, Broad Street Pressure Wash provides dependable exterior window cleaning throughout Charleston and surrounding communities.

Lowcountry Conditions

Window Cleaning in Charleston’s Coastal Climate

Salt in the air near the water dries on glass as a cloudy film, and it gets worse every week it is left there. FAQ

Exterior Window Cleaning Charleston FAQ

Common questions before booking exterior window cleaning in Charleston, SC.

Do you clean upper-floor windows?

Yes. We use a water-fed carbon-fiber pole that reaches second- and third-story exterior windows from the ground, so we don't need to lean ladders against fragile siding, gutters, or trim.

How is exterior window cleaning different from interior?

Exterior cleaning addresses only the outside of the glass — pollen, salt residue, water spots, and coastal haze. If the inside of the glass also has dust, fingerprints, or film, our interior window cleaning service covers that and can be booked in the same visit.

How often should exterior windows be cleaned in Charleston, SC?

Most Charleston homes benefit from a clean once or twice a year. Coastal-facing or heavily shaded properties often need more frequent service because of salt air and pollen.

Do you serve areas outside Charleston?

Yes — Mount Pleasant, North Charleston, James Island, Johns Island, Daniel Island, and Summerville are all in our service area.
