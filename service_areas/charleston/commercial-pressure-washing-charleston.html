Service Focus

Commercial Cleaning That Matches the Surface

We bring two methods to every commercial job. High-pressure surface cleaning handles concrete sidewalks, brick pads, dumpster enclosures, parking surfaces, and other hard surfaces that can take real pressure. Soft washing handles painted stucco, EIFS, awnings, signage areas, and other surfaces that would be damaged by high pressure — a low-pressure cleaning solution lifts algae and mildew at the source without etching paint or punching through delicate coatings.

For multi-surface jobs (a storefront with sidewalk, awning, and stucco all visible from the street), we walk the property first so we know which method goes where before we turn anything on.

FAQ

Commercial Pressure Washing Charleston FAQ

Common questions Charleston business owners, property managers, and HOA boards ask before scheduling commercial pressure washing.

What types of commercial properties do you service in Charleston?

We service Charleston-area storefronts, restaurants and bars, office buildings, multi-tenant retail, HOA-managed buildings and amenity areas, shopping centers, salons and spas, medical and dental offices, and industrial or warehouse exteriors.

Do you offer after-hours or before-open commercial cleaning?

Yes. Many commercial jobs are scheduled overnight or before opening so storefronts, restaurants, and offices stay open for business during the day. Tell us your operating hours and we'll schedule around them.

Do you provide certificates of insurance for commercial jobs?

Yes. We're fully insured and can provide a Certificate of Insurance (COI) on request before work begins. Property managers and HOA boards typically request this for vendor compliance.

Can you set up recurring or contracted commercial service?

Yes. Many Charleston commercial customers run quarterly, monthly, or seasonal cleaning schedules — sidewalks and storefronts after the busy tourist season, dumpster pads on a fixed cadence, parking surfaces twice a year. We build a written schedule that fits the property.

What's the difference between commercial pressure washing and soft washing?

Commercial pressure washing uses high pressure for hard surfaces — sidewalks, parking lots, dumpster pads, concrete walls. Commercial soft washing uses low pressure with a cleaning solution for delicate surfaces — stucco, painted facades, awnings, storefront panels. Most commercial jobs use both methods on different surfaces of the same property.

Do you provide commercial pressure washing near me outside Charleston?

Yes. Broad Street Pressure Wash also services commercial properties in Mount Pleasant, North Charleston, James Island, Johns Island, Daniel Island, Summerville, and Goose Creek.
