Soft Wash Roof Cleaning That Restores a Cleaner Exterior Look

Professional soft wash roof cleaning brightens the roofline and improves the appearance of the entire home. It is a valuable service for homeowners who care about curb appeal, routine exterior maintenance, and protecting the visual condition of the property.

How soft washing works: we apply a low-pressure cleaning solution — a diluted sodium hypochlorite mix with a surfactant — that kills the algae and lichen at the root. Know the Signs

Signs Your Roof Needs Cleaning

  • Black or dark gray streaks running down the shingles, usually on the north slope first
  • Green moss growing in the shaded valleys or along the edges of the roof
  • Crusty, pale green or white patches of lichen stuck to shingles or metal panels
  • Granules and organic debris collecting in the gutters below
  • Uneven color across the roof that makes the home look older than it is

Lowcountry Conditions

Charleston Weather and Your Roof

The black streaks on Lowcountry roofs are a living algae, not dirt. Warm temperatures, frequent summer storms and humidity give it what it needs most of the year, and shade from live oaks and pines keeps the roof wet longer after each rain. Pollen and leaf debris that settle in the valleys hold more moisture and feed moss and lichen.

Homes close to the water also get a fine salt film on metal roofs and flashing. Standing-seam metal roofs are common on Lowcountry homes and need the same gentle treatment as shingles: no high pressure, just the right solution and a careful rinse.

FAQ

Roof Cleaning Charleston FAQ

These are common questions homeowners ask before booking roof cleaning in Charleston, SC.

What causes black streaks on roofs in Charleston?

Black streaks on Charleston roofs are caused by Gloeocapsa magma, a hardy blue-green algae that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. Charleston's combination of high humidity (typically 70%+), warm temperatures, and frequent rainfall creates ideal conditions for it to spread, and the north- and shaded sides of the roof always streak first because algae prefers low UV exposure. The dark color is from a protective sheath the algae produces to shield itself from sunlight — it's not dirt, and it won't rinse off with rain.

How often should a roof be cleaned in Charleston, SC?

Most Charleston roofs need professional cleaning every 2–3 years. Homes under heavy oak or pine canopy, on shaded lots, or near tidal marshes often need it every 18–24 months because the algae returns faster. Homes in sunnier, more exposed locations — typical of newer subdivisions in West Ashley, Daniel Island, and Mount Pleasant — can usually go 3–4 years between washes. A good rule of thumb: if streaks are visible from the street, the roof is overdue.

How does soft wash roof cleaning work?

Soft wash roof cleaning uses a low-pressure spray (under 100 PSI — less than a garden hose) combined with a diluted sodium hypochlorite cleaning solution and a surfactant that helps the mix cling to the shingle. The chemistry kills the algae, lichen, and moss at the root rather than blasting it off with water, so asphalt granules, flashing, and roof sealants stay intact. Results brighten over the days following the wash as rain rinses the treatment through the shingles. This is the method recommended by the Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association — high-pressure washing voids most shingle warranties.

How much does roof cleaning cost in Charleston?

Most Charleston roof cleaning jobs run $400–$800 depending on roof size, pitch, height, and how much black streaking, lichen, and moss has built up. A single-story ranch with mild streaking is on the lower end; a two-story home with steep pitch and heavy north-side algae is on the higher end. Broad Street Pressure Wash provides a free walk-around quote before any work starts.

Is roof cleaning safe for asphalt shingles?

Yes — when it's soft washed, not pressure washed. Major shingle manufacturers — GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed — explicitly recommend the soft-wash method using a diluted sodium-hypochlorite solution in their published roof-cleaning guidance. High-pressure washing, by contrast, strips the protective granules off the shingle surface, shortens roof life, and often voids the warranty. Broad Street Pressure Wash uses the manufacturer-approved soft-wash method on every Charleston roof.
