Gutter & Downspout Service

Professional Gutter Cleaning for Charleston Homes

Gutters in Charleston collect more than just leaves. Pine straw, roof grit, seed pods, storm debris, and damp organic buildup can all pack into the gutter line and stop water from draining the way it should. Once that happens, overflow can spill down the siding, pool near the foundation, and create a mess around entryways, beds, and walkways.

Professional gutter cleaning helps keep the roofline functioning properly while reducing the risk of water spilling where it should not. For many homeowners, it is one of the most practical services for protecting the exterior and staying ahead of preventable maintenance issues.

Whether your gutters are visibly overflowing, packed with debris, or simply overdue for routine service, Broad Street Pressure Wash provides dependable gutter cleaning throughout Charleston and surrounding areas.

One quick note on scope: we provide gutter cleaning — debris removal, downspout flushing, and exterior gutter face brightening. We do not install new gutters, repair pulled-away gutter sections, or replace damaged gutter line. If your gutters need installation or structural repair, we'll point you toward a Charleston gutter contractor; if they just need to be cleared and brightened, that's what we do.

Know the Signs

Signs Your Gutters Need Cleaning

  • Water spilling over the gutter edge during summer storms
  • Plants or seedlings growing out of the gutter line
  • Downspouts that drip slowly or not at all after rain
  • Dark stripes ("tiger stripes") on the outside face of the gutters
  • Puddles or washed-out mulch near the foundation

Lowcountry Conditions

Pine Straw, Oak Leaves and Charleston Downpours

Lowcountry gutters fill up all year: pine straw and pollen in spring, oak tassels and leaves in fall, and twigs after every big storm. Charleston summer storms can drop heavy rain very fast, so a half-clogged gutter overflows quickly and sends water against siding, windows and the foundation.

Wet debris left in a gutter also feeds the algae and moss on the roof edge above it. We clear gutters by hand, flush the downspouts to make sure water moves, bag the debris and can brighten the gutter faces while we are there. This is a cleaning service only. We do not install or repair gutters.

FAQ

Gutter Cleaning Charleston FAQ

These are common questions homeowners ask before booking gutter cleaning in Charleston, SC.

What does gutter cleaning include?

A standard gutter cleaning visit includes hand-removing leaves, pine straw, seed pods, and packed roof debris from every gutter channel; flushing each downspout with water until it runs clear; bagging the debris and removing it from the property; and a quick exterior brightening of the gutter face so the roofline looks as clean as the rest of the home. We're a cleaning-only service — we don't install new gutters, repair pulled-away sections, or replace damaged gutter line.

How often should gutters be cleaned in Charleston, SC?

Most Charleston homes need gutter cleaning twice a year — once in spring (April–May, after oak pollen and seed-pod drop) and once in fall (October–November, after leaf drop). Homes under heavy pine canopy or shaded by live oaks often need quarterly cleaning because pine needles clog faster and are heavier when wet. Homes in newer subdivisions with less mature tree cover can sometimes get by with one annual cleaning, scheduled before hurricane season.

How much does gutter cleaning cost in Charleston?

Most Charleston gutter cleaning jobs run $150–$350 depending on home size, gutter linear footage, number of stories, and how much debris is in the system. A single-story home with 150 feet of gutter and light debris is on the lower end; a two-story home with 250+ feet of gutter and a season's worth of pine straw is on the higher end. Broad Street Pressure Wash provides a free walk-around quote — no charge to look at the property and price the work.

Why is gutter cleaning important in Charleston?

Clogged gutters in Charleston cause more damage than in drier climates because the Lowcountry gets 50+ inches of rain a year, much of it in heavy summer downpours. When water can't drain through the downspouts, it overflows the front lip of the gutter — running down siding (streaking and rotting it), pooling near the foundation (where Charleston's high water table compounds the problem), and saturating wood trim and fascia (the most common cause of soft-spot rot on Lowcountry homes). Clean gutters are the cheapest way to protect a Charleston home from preventable water damage.

Do you clean gutters near me — outside Charleston?

Yes. Broad Street Pressure Wash provides gutter cleaning throughout Mount Pleasant, North Charleston, James Island, Johns Island, Daniel Island, Summerville, Goose Creek, West Ashley, Folly Beach, Sullivan's Island, and Isle of Palms. Most jobs are scheduled within the same week the quote is requested.

What's the difference between gutter cleaning and gutter installation?

Gutter cleaning is the maintenance service we provide — clearing debris from existing gutters and downspouts so water drains the way it should. Gutter installation is a separate trade: installing new gutter line, replacing damaged sections, or repairing pulled-away gutters. If your gutters need installation or structural repair, we'll point you toward a Charleston gutter contractor. If they just need to be cleared and brightened, that's our work.
