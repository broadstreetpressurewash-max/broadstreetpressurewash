Charleston Pressure Washing FAQ

Questions Charleston Homeowners Ask

Which pressure washing service does my Charleston home need most?

For most Charleston homes the four highest-impact services are house soft wash (siding and trim), driveway cleaning (concrete and aggregate), gutter cleaning (debris removal), and roof cleaning (black streaks). Exterior and interior window cleaning are typically scheduled alongside or as a separate visit. We bundle multiple services into one visit when it makes sense for the property.

What's the difference between pressure washing, power washing, and soft washing?

Pressure washing and power washing both refer to high-pressure water cleaning of hard surfaces like driveways, concrete, and gutter faces — the two terms are used interchangeably in Charleston. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution to lift algae and mildew from delicate surfaces like roofs, siding, soffits, and stucco. We use whichever method is right for each surface.

How often should each service be done in Charleston?

In the Lowcountry climate, most homeowners schedule house soft wash and driveway cleaning yearly, gutter cleaning twice a year (spring and fall), roof soft washing every 2–3 years, and window cleaning twice a year. Heavily shaded homes or properties under oak canopies often need more frequent service.

Can I bundle multiple services into one visit?

Yes. Bundling house wash, driveway, gutter, and roof cleaning into one visit is common for Charleston customers and is often the most efficient way to refresh the full exterior of a home. Request a free quote and we'll price the bundle together.
